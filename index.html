Academic Project Documentation

About ShikshaShare

ShikshaShare was built as a college Community Engagement Project (CEP) to solve a persistent educational challenge: students each year struggle to afford essential textbooks, scientific tools, and drafting instruments, while thousands of usable items sit idle in lockers and homes after semester exams.

The Challenge We Address

Technical textbooks cost ₹500–₹1200, scientific calculators exceed ₹1500, and engineering drafting sets are expensive for a single semester of coursework. At the same time, children supported by local municipal schools and community NGOs often lack basic geometry boxes and sturdy backpacks.

By the end of each semester, graduating or advancing students have little practical way to pass these materials forward safely without commercial exploitation or disposal.

The ShikshaShare Solution

ShikshaShare connects the academic ecosystem into a cooperative resource loop. Students can find what they need, providers can offer items at affordable nominal prices, and donors can give usable materials directly to students or pledge to active NGO drives.

Partnered with NSS college units and registered foundations, the platform ensures verified delivery to students who need them most.

Core Principles of the Platform

Guiding values behind the ShikshaShare Community Engagement Project.

1. Educational Access

Every learner deserves access to required syllabus textbooks, instruments, and equipment regardless of financial background.

2. Circular Reuse

Prolonging the life of study materials reduces waste, saves paper, and prevents premature disposal of durable equipment.

3. Community Solidarity

College NSS volunteers and community NGOs coordinate collection and distribution so assistance reaches verified beneficiaries.

4. Trust & Non-Commercial

Unlike commercial classifieds, ShikshaShare is designed purely for academic utility, free donations, and mutual student help.
